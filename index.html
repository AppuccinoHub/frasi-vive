How Frasi Vive works

  • Move: swipe up, scroll, use the ↑ ↓ keys, or the Next button.
  • Each card: Listen, then build or type the Italian phrase, then Say it out loud.
  • Levels: Level 2 unlocks at 3 of 4 correct in Level 1; Level 3 unlocks at 3 of 4 in Level 2. Missed cards come back.
  • Streak: cards right in a row, first try, no hint.
  • Privacy: no login, no names. Progress is kept only in this browser. Voice recordings never leave the device and are never saved.
  • Songs: previews stream from Apple Music when you tap Play (courtesy of Apple Music). If your network blocks them, the card still works.
